Nothing is uploaded
This page is served with a policy that lets it connect only to parallelogram.dev. Your file is read by code running in the tab and goes nowhere.
The same verdict
The engine is the released Python package, unmodified. What exits 0 here exits 0 in your terminal and in CI.
Not for everything
A tab is slower than a terminal. In our tests 60,000 records (25 MB) took about 20 seconds to check here and 300,000 (123 MB) about a minute and a half. The CLI is roughly 2.5 times faster and streams.
